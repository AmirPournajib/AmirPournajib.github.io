Looking ahead

My take on the Rivian app today

I read owner reviews and forum threads to understand where the app delights people and where it lets them down. The pattern is familiar: when the link to the device is weak, the whole product feels weak.

What works well

  • Frequent updates that owners notice and appreciate.
  • The new Apple Watch app: lock, climate and charge target from the wrist, with up to four quick controls.
  • Passive entry with the Gen 2 digital key: walk up and the vehicle unlocks.
  • Consistent icons for Climate, Energy and Gear Guard across phone and watch.

Where owners struggle

  • Commands stall at "waking vehicle", and some owners say remote access works only part of the time.
  • Commands often work only when the phone is close enough for Bluetooth.
  • The phone as key can feel slow.
  • Requests for home-screen widgets, door and window status, and charging history.
Three things I would explore first

1. Make every remote command honest about its state

Show each step (sent, waking vehicle, done) with the typical wait time, retry on its own, and switch to Bluetooth automatically when the owner is nearby. A clear "still trying" beats a silent failure.

Measure: command success rate, p95 command latency (time the slowest 5% take), retries per command
From my work: at HealthGauge every result needed a cloud round trip, and the app had to show progress so people trusted the answer.

2. Show how fresh every number is

Put "updated 2 minutes ago" next to charge, range and lock state, and mark stale data clearly. Owners forgive old data; they do not forgive data that looks current and is wrong.

Measure: share of sessions showing stale status, time since last vehicle update
From my work: when the watch lost its connection, gaps in readings did more damage to trust than any single bad number.

3. Bring the vehicle to the home screen

Widgets for battery, lock state and climate, chosen by the owner, so the most common checks never need the app to open and wake the vehicle.

Measure: widget adoption, app opens per owner, vehicle wake-ups per day
