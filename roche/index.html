HealthGauge · Connected device, mobile apps, cloud AI

Cuffless blood pressure on a smartwatch

iOS, Android and web apps that paired with a third-party watch over Bluetooth, calibrated each user against a standard cuff, and used a cloud AI model to estimate blood pressure from ECG and PPG signals.

Product ManagerEdmonton, ABNov 2019 to Oct 2022
OverviewArchitectureBuildTeam and deliveryPrototype to productResults
01The problem

Blood pressure changes all day. A cuff sees one moment.

A cuff reading is accurate but occasional, and many people never take one at home. A watch is worn all day and already records heart signals. The goal: turn those signals into blood pressure estimates that consumers and researchers could trust.

02Architecture

From the watch to a number people trust

Press play, or pick a step. Try "Simulate a dropped connection" to see why connection reliability shaped the product.

ECG: electrical heart signal. PPG: optical pulse signal from the watch's light sensor. PTT (pulse transit time) and PAT (pulse arrival time): how long the pulse takes to travel, which changes with blood pressure. The phone screens are illustrations with example values, not the original app.

Product

Measure on the phone, review on the web

The phone shows heart rate, blood pressure and HRV with live ECG and PPG traces and a signal-quality bar during each measurement. The web dashboard shows blood pressure history, the raw signals behind each reading, heart rate trends, daily lowest resting heart rate and activity, for users and research teams.

Users choose what matters to them

Home-screen widgets for HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, steps, temperature, blood oxygen and more, each one switched on or off by the user.

Texavie · Hardware, firmware, mobile, AI

Smart glove and knee sleeve sensor hub

A washable smart glove and knee sleeve that track hand, finger and knee movement for rehab clinics, athletes, researchers and consumers. I led the hardware and firmware team and designed the hub that turns 32 sensor channels into data on a phone.

Technical Product Manager, Hardware and Firmware LeadVancouver, BCOct 2022 to Mar 2024
OverviewArchitectureBuildTeam and deliveryPrototype to productResults
01The problem

Measure movement without cameras

Rehab and sports teams needed to measure hand, finger and knee movement outside a lab. Camera systems are expensive and fixed in place. A comfortable, washable wearable could capture the same movement anywhere, if the hardware could stream enough data without draining the battery.

02Architecture

From textile sensors to AI insight

Diagram drawn from my own design. The push button and LEDs sit in the center of the board so they line up with the enclosure.

03Build

Every track I coordinated

Hardware

  • Designed the hub from schematic and PCB layout to the enclosed product
  • Nordic nRF module (MCU and Bluetooth) as the brain
  • Two Analog Devices SPI ADCs reading 32 channels through one flat connector
  • Humidity and temperature sensors to protect the electronics

Connectivity and power

  • Bluetooth Low Energy data rate balanced against battery life
  • Enough data for the AI models, enough battery for a full session
  • Kept traffic to the cloud as small as possible

Mechanical and enclosure

  • Push button and LEDs placed in the center of the board to line up with the case
  • Worked with mechanical engineers on the enclosure and wearables

Software and cloud

  • Phone app receiving live sensor data
  • Firebase for the cloud backend and data
  • Data pipeline feeding model training

AI

  • Models for hand gesture detection and muscle strength
  • Turned raw sensor data into insights for clinics and athletes

Manufacturing and suppliers

  • Stage-gate reviews from concept to pilot builds
  • Part lead times, supplier coordination and pilot builds
  • Scaled to a 5,000-unit production run

How I work

The product management side

Turning ideas into shipped, measured features

Alongside the technical work, I own the product craft: what to build, in what order, and how we know it worked.

PRIORITIZEWhat goes in first

Decisions a technical product manager owns

What I weigh at every stage

Bandwidth, data rate and battery

Bluetooth is limited and every transfer costs power. Send what the product needs, at the rate it needs, and no more.

Device or cloud

Process on the device when it can, so less data travels back and forth to the server. Use the cloud for what truly needs it.

Cloud platform and data

Choose the platform and database that fit the product and team, such as Azure, AWS or Firebase, and plan how data is stored and used.

Security and compliance

HIPAA and PIPEDA for health data, with privacy and access designed in from the start.

AI agents

Pick the right agent for each task, test that it does the job well, and give each one a clear owner.

Third-party devices and APIs

Make sure partner hardware and APIs work reliably, including Bluetooth connections to devices we do not control.

Cross-team orchestration

Keep hardware, firmware, mechanical, software, design and AI teams talking, with clear owners for every interface.

Delivery rhythm

Daily stand-ups for small teams, sprints and stage-gates for larger ones, tracked in Jira, Confluence or Monday.com.
